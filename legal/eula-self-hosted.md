**The Instance Operator is the data controller** for all personal data processed by their self-hosted instance of the Software.

- Is not a processor, joint controller, or in any other data-processing relationship with respect to data on self-hosted instances.
- Provides the software "as is" for the Instance Operator to deploy and operate.

**The Instance Operator is solely responsible for:**
- Complying with applicable data protection law (including GDPR) for their instance.
- Obtaining any necessary regulatory authorisations (including any AISP authorisation if required by the PSD2 analysis — see the Developer's compliance notes in `psd2-role-note.md`).
- Signing required Data Processing Agreements with any processors they use (Enable Banking, GlitchTip, Brevo, etc.).
- Maintaining the security of their server, database encryption, and key management.
- Providing users of their instance with appropriate privacy notices and terms.
- Handling data-subject rights requests (access, erasure, portability) for users of their instance.
- Reporting data breaches to the relevant supervisory authority.
